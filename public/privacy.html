Privacy Policy

Effective 20 September 2026 · Last updated 20 September 2026

Paper Archive (pa.solidstart.jp, "the Service") is operated by SolidStart, Jōetsu, Niigata, Japan ("we", "us"). Contact: privacy@solidstart.jp. This policy explains what data the Service collects, why, where it is stored, who processes it, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it.

1. What the Service does

You photograph a paper document — mail, a bill, a receipt, a school letter. The Service reads the text from the photo, works out what the document is and what it asks of you (a payment, a signature, a deadline), labels it, itemises receipts into a spending record, stores the photo, and keeps a searchable index so you can find the document again after the paper is gone. It also suggests whether the paper original is worth keeping.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Your Google account

You sign in with Google. From Google we receive your account identifier, email address and display name, and we store them. We request only the basic sign-in scopes (openid, email, profile). We do not request or access Google Drive, Gmail, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google data.

2.2 Documents you upload

  • The photo or PDF you upload, stored unchanged.
  • Text recognised from it (OCR).
  • Data derived from it: title, sender, dates, amounts, reference numbers, document type, topic labels, what action it requires and by when, whether the original is worth keeping, keywords, tables found in the document, and for receipts and bills the merchant, totals, tax, payment method and individual line items with a category (for example groceries, snacks, household).
  • Corrections you make: a sender you type in, a retention decision you set.
  • A history of each automated reading of the document, so a later re-analysis never loses earlier results.

Documents often contain personal data about you and about other people (names, addresses, account numbers, amounts). You are responsible for what you upload; upload only documents you are entitled to keep.

2.3 Archives and sharing

Every user has one archive. You can invite other people into your archive with a link; anyone who accepts sees every document in that archive, including the photos, and can add documents to it. We store who is a member of which archive, who created each invite, and who added each document.

2.4 Usage and cost records

For each document reading we record when it happened, which archive it belonged to, the size of the upload, how many pages were read, how many model tokens were used and what that cost, and how long it took. These records are used for operating the Service and understanding its costs. When a document or account is deleted, its cost records are kept but the link to the document and the account is removed, so they no longer identify you.

2.5 Technical data

  • Cookies: a signed session cookie that keeps you signed in, and a short-lived cookie used during Google sign-in. Both are essential; there are no advertising or analytics cookies.
  • On your device: your language choice and, for operators, an API token, kept in the browser's local storage; the app shell is cached for offline loading. Nothing else is stored on the device.
  • Server logs: request logs (IP address, user agent, time, path, status) kept by our hosting provider for security and debugging, with short retention. Error logs contain a request identifier, not document contents.

We do not use analytics services, advertising identifiers or tracking pixels.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: read, interpret, label, store, search and display your documents; show deadlines; summarise spending.
  • To let you share an archive with people you invite.
  • To keep the Service secure: sign-in, access control (only members of an archive can open its documents), abuse prevention.
  • To operate and improve the Service: monitoring errors, measuring cost per document, and improving how documents are read. We do not use your documents to train machine-learning models, and our processors are contractually prevented from doing so.

We do not show advertising, and we do not sell, rent or trade personal data.

4. Google user data

Paper Archive's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically: the Google account identifier, email address and name are used only to sign you in, to show who you are to other members of an archive you share, and to manage access to the Service. They are not used for advertising, are not sold, and are not transferred to anyone except as needed to provide the Service or as required by law.

6. Where data is stored and international transfers

We are based in Japan. Your data is processed by the providers above in Singapore, the United States and on Cloudflare's network. Where data leaves Japan we rely on the providers' contractual commitments and security measures described in their terms.

7. How long we keep data

  • Documents (photo, text, derived data, reading history): until you delete the document or your account.
  • Account and archive membership: until you delete your account.
  • Invite links: 7 days, or until revoked.
  • Usage and cost records: kept, without the link to the deleted document or account.
  • Server logs: short retention set by the hosting provider, typically days.

8. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Photos and database contents are encrypted at rest by the providers. Access to a document requires a valid session for a member of its archive; photos are never served from a public location, and temporary links to a photo expire within minutes. Sessions are signed and cannot be forged. Credentials for our providers are kept in a secrets manager, never in code. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will inform you.

9. Your choices and rights

  • See and delete documents in the app at any time. Deleting a document removes the photo, the text, the derived data and the reading history.
  • Leave an archive someone shared with you, or remove members from your own.
  • Delete your account and all your data by emailing privacy@solidstart.jp from your sign-in address; we delete within 30 days and confirm.
  • Revoke Google sign-in at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Access, correction, portability: email us and we will provide a copy of your data in a common format or correct it.

Where the law of your country grants further rights (for example under the Act on the Protection of Personal Information in Japan, or the GDPR), you may exercise them through the same contact address.

10. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Access is by invitation only.

11. Retention advice is advice

The Service suggests whether a paper original can be discarded. The suggestion is deliberately conservative and is not legal, tax or financial advice. When the Service is unsure it says so, and the decision is yours.

12. Changes

When this policy changes we update this page and the dates at the top. Material changes are announced in the app.

13. Contact

1. 本サービスの内容

郵便物、請求書、レシート、学校のお知らせなど、紙の書類をスマホで撮っていただきます。本サービスは、その写真から文字を読み取り、何の書類で、何を求めているか（支払い、署名、期限など）を判断してラベルを付け、レシートは明細ごとに支出の記録にまとめます。写真を保存し、検索できる記録を残すので、紙を手放したあとでもその書類を見つけられます。あわせて、紙の原本を取っておく価値があるかどうかの目安もお伝えします。

2. 取得する情報

2.1 Googleアカウント

ログインにはGoogleアカウントを使います。Googleからはアカウントの識別子、メールアドレス、表示名を受け取り、これらを保存します。要求するのはログインのための基本的な範囲（openid、email、profile）だけです。Googleドライブ、Gmail、連絡先、カレンダーなど、その他のGoogleのデータを要求することも、参照することもありません。

2.2 アップロードされた書類

  • アップロードされた写真またはPDF（変更せずそのまま保存します）
  • そこから読み取った文字（OCR）
  • 読み取った内容から導き出した情報：タイトル、差出人、日付、金額、整理番号、書類の種類、内容を表すラベル、必要な手続きとその期限、原本を取っておく価値があるかどうか、キーワード、書類に含まれる表。レシートと請求書については、店舗名、合計金額、税額、支払い方法、および分類（食料品、菓子、日用品など）を付けた明細の一件ごとの情報
  • お客様による修正（入力し直した差出人、ご自身で設定した保管の要否など）
  • 書類ごとの自動読み取りの履歴（あとから読み取り直しても、それまでの結果が失われないようにするためです）

書類には、お客様ご自身や他の方の個人情報（氏名、住所、口座番号、金額など）が含まれていることがよくあります。何をアップロードするかはお客様の責任です。ご自身が保管してよい書類だけをアップロードしてください。

2.3 アーカイブと共有

お客様おひとりにつき、アーカイブは1つです。リンクを送って、ほかの方をご自身のアーカイブに招待できます。招待を受け入れた方は、そのアーカイブにあるすべての書類を写真も含めて見ることができ、書類を追加することもできます。本サービスは、誰がどのアーカイブのメンバーか、どの招待を誰が作成したか、どの書類を誰が追加したかを保存します。

2.4 利用状況と費用の記録

書類を1件読み取るごとに、実行した日時、対象のアーカイブ、アップロードされたデータの大きさ、読み取ったページ数、使用したモデルのトークン数とその費用、処理にかかった時間を記録します。これらは本サービスの運営と、かかる費用の把握に使います。書類やアカウントが削除されたあとも費用の記録は残りますが、書類やアカウントとのひも付けは外すため、個人を特定できる状態ではなくなります。

Cookie：ログイン状態を保つための署名付きのセッションCookieと、Googleでのログインの間だけ使う短期間のCookieを使います。どちらも本サービスの提供に欠かせないものです。広告やアクセス解析のためのCookieは使いません。
