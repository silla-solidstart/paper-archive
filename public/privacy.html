What Paper Archive does

You photograph a paper document. We read the text from it, work out what the document is and what it asks of you, store a copy in your Google Drive, and keep a searchable index so you can find it later.

What we store, and where

  • The document itself is stored in your own Google Drive, in a folder named "Paper Archive" that this app creates. We do not keep a copy of the image or PDF on our servers.
  • The text and metadata extracted from each document — the recognised text, title, issuer, dates, amounts, what action it requires, and whether the original is likely worth keeping — are stored in our database so that search and reminders work.
  • Your account: the Google account identifier, email address and name you sign in with, and the tokens needed to write to your Drive folder. Refresh tokens are stored encrypted.

Google Drive access

We request the narrowest Drive permission Google offers (drive.file). It lets this app see and manage only the files it created. It does not give us access to anything else in your Drive.

Processing partners

To read and understand documents we send them to Google Cloud Document AI (text recognition) and Anthropic's Claude (interpretation). These providers process the data to provide the service and do not use it to train their models under the terms we use. Data is processed in Singapore (Document AI) and the United States (Anthropic).

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not read your Drive beyond the files this app created.
  • We do not use your documents to train models.

Deleting your data

Deleting a document in the app removes it from our index and moves the Drive copy to your Drive trash. To delete your account and all indexed data, email the contact address above; we remove it within 30 days. You can revoke the app's Drive access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Retention advice is advice

The app suggests whether a paper original can be discarded. It is a suggestion, deliberately conservative, and not legal or tax advice. When unsure, the app says so, and you decide.

Changes

We will update this page and the effective date when the policy changes.
